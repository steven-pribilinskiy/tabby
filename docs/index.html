How to read this

Every entry names the commits it is made of, and its +/− numbers come from git's own diffstat for exactly those commits — not from an estimate. features.js is the single source the cards, the filters and the detail pages all read, so they cannot disagree with each other.

Where a feature is a fix to something upstream already built, it says so. Where something is not proven, it says that too, in its own block on the page. The fork's working notes (CLAUDE.md) are unusually candid about what has and has not been measured, and a catalogue that quietly dropped that would be worse than no catalogue at all.

No screenshots are shipped. The pages support them and will show them per theme when they exist; nothing here fakes one in the meantime.

Known limits

Things this fork does not fix, or has not proved it fixes

  • Emoji width is still wrong. ❇️ gets one column where Windows Terminal gives two, so the glyph paints wider than the cell reserved for it and everything after it sits one column off. xterm 6 removed the first blocker, but the addon that carries the fix — @xterm/addon-unicode-graphemes@0.4.0 — cannot be put in this bundle at all: importing it spins the renderer at 100% CPU during module evaluation, and that is still unexplained. This is a known issue, not a feature.
  • The stale-glyph artifacts are not reproduced. The test written to measure them reports 0 dirty cells on the canvas renderer, on WebGL and on xterm 6 alike, so it does not reproduce the artifacts that prompted the renderer work and cannot be cited as proof they are fixed. Retiring the canvas renderer is well-founded on its own grounds.
  • Some things are upstream's, and are not claimed here. Separate light and dark colour schemes, and pane titles with draggable dividers, are stock upstream Tabby. So is the jump list itself — this fork only fixed the icons on it.
  • Not everything on the branch is in the catalogue. Six commits are deliberately absent: two binding.gyp patches that only exist so the native modules compile on this machine, two pure CLAUDE.md commits, and a reverted first attempt at the xterm 6 upgrade together with its revert.
